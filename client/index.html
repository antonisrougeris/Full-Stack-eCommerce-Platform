FAQ

Questions? We scanned them first.

Everything you need to know about Skanare products, dynamic QR codes, delivery and returns.

What is a dynamic QR code?

A dynamic QR code keeps the same physical printed code, while allowing the online destination behind it to be updated later.

Can I change where my QR links after purchase?

Yes. Eligible Skanare dynamic QR products allow you to change the destination URL without replacing the printed QR code.

What can I link my QR code to?

You can link it to supported online destinations such as a social profile, website, portfolio, event page, business page or other valid URL.

Is every QR code unique?

Yes. Dynamic QR-enabled products are associated with their own unique QR identifier.

Does my QR code expire?

The printed QR itself does not need to change when you update its destination. Continued online redirect functionality depends on the Skanare service remaining available.

What happens if I enter the wrong link?

For supported dynamic QR products, you can update the destination later without changing the physical QR print.

How do I manage my purchased QR codes?

Sign in to your Skanare account and go to My QR Codes to view the QR products associated with your account and manage their destinations.
