What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information that websites may store in your browser to support functionality, remember preferences and improve the user experience.

Essential storage

Skanare may use cookies or browser storage mechanisms required for essential functionality such as authentication, security and shopping cart behaviour.

Local browser storage

Certain features may use local browser storage to remember temporary information, preferences or navigation state.

Third-party services

Third-party services integrated into the website may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

Examples may include services used for payment processing, customer support or analytics.

Managing cookies

Most browsers allow users to remove or restrict cookies through browser settings.

Blocking essential storage technologies may prevent some Skanare features from working correctly.

Changes to this policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated when the technologies or services used by Skanare change.
