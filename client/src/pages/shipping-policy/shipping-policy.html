SHIPPING POLICY

From Skanare to your door.

Information about order preparation, shipping costs and delivery.

Last updated: September 2026

Order preparation

Orders begin processing after successful payment confirmation.

Some products may already be prepared and available in stock, while others may require additional production time.

Shipping costs

Any applicable shipping charge is shown during checkout before payment.

Shipping costs may vary depending on destination, delivery method and other fulfilment factors.

Delivery times

Any delivery estimate provided by Skanare should be considered an estimate rather than a guaranteed delivery date unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Courier delays, holidays, weather conditions and other circumstances outside our reasonable control may affect delivery.

Shipping information

Please make sure your delivery name, address, postal code and telephone number are entered correctly during checkout.

Incorrect or incomplete shipping information can cause delays or unsuccessful delivery attempts.

Tracking and delivery updates

Where tracking information is available, it may be provided after the order has been handed to the delivery provider.

Delivery problems

If your shipment appears delayed, lost or damaged, contact us and include your order number.

Delivery question?

Email hello@skanare.com and include your order number.
