ABOUT NOVASTORE

Wear the connection.

NovaStore creates modern apparel and accessories that connect the physical world with your digital identity.

Not just a brand. A code.

NovaStore was created around a simple idea: everyday products can do more than just look good. They can connect people instantly to something meaningful.

Our products combine clean design with unique QR technology, turning clothing and accessories into a direct bridge between the physical and digital world.

Your QR. Your destination.

Selected NovaStore products include a unique modern commerce code. The printed QR remains the same, while its destination can be updated later.

That means you can connect your product to your social media profile, website, portfolio, business, event, campaign or another supported online destination.

Built to stay useful.

Traditional printed products are usually fixed. With a dynamic NovaStore QR, the physical code does not need to change when your digital destination changes.

Your product can therefore evolve together with you.

Designed for real people.

NovaStore is built for creators, brands, events, businesses and anyone who wants to connect what they wear with what they want to share.

Customer account. Your link. Your story.

One physical product. A destination you control.
