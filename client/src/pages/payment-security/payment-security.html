PAYMENT & SECURITY

Safe checkout. Secure experience.

We designed the Skanare checkout experience to keep payment processing and account access secure and straightforward.

Secure online payments

Card payments on Skanare are processed through Viva.com using its secure checkout environment.

Skanare does not need to store your complete payment card number in order to process and fulfil your purchase.

Payment confirmation

An order is treated as successfully paid only after payment confirmation is received through our payment processing system.

Once payment is confirmed, the order can proceed to fulfilment and any eligible dynamic QR product can be assigned to your account.

Account security

Customer accounts are protected through authenticated access.

Certain account functions, including management of purchased QR codes, are available only to the authenticated account associated with those products.

Your dynamic QR

Each eligible dynamic QR product is associated with a unique QR identifier.

Updating the destination URL does not require replacing the QR printed on your physical Skanare product.

Suspicious activity

If you believe someone has gained unauthorised access to your Skanare account, change your password and contact us as soon as possible.
